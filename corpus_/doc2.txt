In Salesforce, lead-related communication such as emails and calls is stored
as Activity records linked to the Lead object.
Email messages can be automatically captured using email-to-Salesforce
or through integration with external email systems.

Activities provide a timeline of interactions between sales representatives
and potential customers.
This allows teams to understand engagement history and follow up effectively.

Salesforce stores metadata such as timestamps, owners, and status changes
to support automation, reporting, and sales performance tracking.
