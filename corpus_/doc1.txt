Salesforce uses the Lead object to store information about potential customers.
A lead typically includes contact details, company information, source, and status.
Leads represent early-stage prospects that have not yet been qualified.

Salesforce allows leads to be created from multiple sources including web forms,
manual entry, marketing campaigns, and API integrations.
Each lead can be tracked through activities such as emails, calls, and tasks.

Once a lead is qualified, it can be converted into an Account, Contact, and Opportunity.
This conversion process preserves historical activity and supports reporting.
Lead management is central to Salesforce sales workflows.
